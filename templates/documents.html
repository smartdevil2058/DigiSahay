Pre-Submission Verification Checklist

Before submitting documents to Jan Seva Kendras or departmental portals, verify these statutory compliance standards:

check_circle
Legibility: Text and photo must be clearly readable. Avoid glare, shadows, or cropped edges.
check_circle
Recent Validity: Utility bills and rent agreements must generally be recent (<3 months for PAN/UIDAI).
check_circle
Name Matching: Spelling on address proof must match applicant name on primary identity card.
